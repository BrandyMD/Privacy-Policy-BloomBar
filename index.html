Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of the BloomBar mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently. This document outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our booking services and maintain app functionality, we collect the following types of information:

a. User-Provided Information

  • Account Registration Details: When you sign up for an account, we collect your email address, password, and optional profile details required to secure and manage your profile.
  • Booking Information: Data related to your transactions, scheduled reservations, and requests submitted through our booking workflow.

b. Automatically Collected Information & Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google to optimize performance, deliver advertisements, and analyze app performance. These services collect data automatically:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Tracks user behavior inside the app, user engagement metrics, session durations, and screen transitions to help us optimize app functionality.
  • Firebase Authentication & Crashlytics: Manages secure user login services and collects anonymous crash reports, stack traces, and device identifiers to troubleshoot software bugs.
  • Google AdMob: Serves contextual or personalized advertisements. AdMob may utilize advertising IDs (such as Android AAID) to manage ad frequency, track conversions, and deliver relevant ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data to fulfill our services and comply with platform rules. Specific uses include:

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing your user account.
  • Processing, confirming, and updating your scheduling and bookings.
  • Analyzing app trends, performance, and stability via analytics to improve features.
  • Displaying promotional materials and advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Enforcing our terms, preventing fraud, and complying with Google Play Console Developer Policies.

3. Data Deletion and Retention Policy

We retain your personal data only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. In strict compliance with Google Play standards, we provide users with absolute control over their account data:

How to Delete Your Data:

You may permanently request the deletion of your account and all associated operational data at any time via two simple methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Use the "Delete Account" option located within your app settings. Pressing this instantly triggers a total wipe of your registration and personal details from our active infrastructure.
  2. Web/Email Request: If you have uninstalled the application, you can submit a manual deletion request by contacting our support channel below. Upon verification, your profile data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top.
